Before finding my way to control theory, I spent quite some time in communication systems. What began as an infatuation during my undergraduate years turned into a rather tumultuous relationship—one that demanded far more than I was willing to give. My master's thesis was perhaps the final chapter in that relationship: a lesson in perseverance, and, more importantly, knowing when to walk away!
