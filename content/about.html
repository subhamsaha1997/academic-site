Hi, I’m Subham! I’m a graduate student at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where I work with Saurabh Amin. My research focuses on control systems, with particular interests in nonlinear control.

I was fortunate to work with two wonderful professors during my undergraduate years: Vineeth BS and Chandra Murthy. They shaped my approach to research and are the reason I first began to imagine a future in academia. In fact, they were the ones who convinced me that I, too, could pursue a PhD!

I earned my undergraduate degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from the Indian Institute of Space Science and Technology. Interestingly, I started out in the Aerospace Department but switched out almost immediately after taking an Engineering Drawing course. Years later, I found my way back to aerospace as a PhD student at MIT.

Before graduate school, I worked as an Embedded Software Developer at the Indian Space Research Organisation. C is the truth.

Outside of research, I enjoy running, cooking, and watching movies.
